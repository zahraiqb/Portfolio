About Me

I am currently a third-year Computer Science student at the University of Edinburgh with an interest in systems engineering and large-scale computational systems.

I enjoy building simulations, prototyping complex systems, and exploring the underlying principles that make modern technology work. I’m particularly interested in understanding systems from the ground up, from their theoretical foundations to the engineering that brings them into the 3D!

I spend much of my free time playing video games, mostly ROBLOX and League of Legends, and exploring theoretical physics through independent research and writing. I also enjoy hands-on experimentation with microcontrollers, Arduinos, and breadboard circuits.
